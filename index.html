Tips for Preparing Your Images and PDFs for Crisp 3D Rendering

To achieve the sharpest visual clarity and fastest loading speeds when converting your files into an interactive book, keep these best practices in mind:

  • Use Consistent Aspect Ratios: If you are combining multiple image files into a single flipbook, try to keep all images in the same orientation (either all portrait or all landscape). Consistent dimensions ensure seamless alignment along the center spine of the book.
  • Name Your Image Files Sequentially: Before selecting multiple photos from your phone or computer, name them sequentially using leading zeros (such as 01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg up to 10.jpg) so your operating system passes them in exact reading order.
  • Optimize Large Print PDFs: Standard digital PDFs exported at 150 DPI to 200 DPI look razor-sharp on Retina smartphones and 4K monitors while rendering almost instantaneously inside mobile browsers.
